## Common questions

**Why does Keeproll need Photos access?**
To find similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos and Live Photos. You can grant access to only some photos; Keeproll scans those. Nothing is uploaded.

**I deleted photos but my free space didn't change.**
iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All to get the space back now.

**A photo I took is listed under "Saved from Chats".**
Keeproll looks for camera metadata, chat-app file names and sizes. Some editing apps strip camera data and can look like a forward. Such photos are capped at "Possible" and are never selected for you. Just don't select it, or mark it a favourite and it won't be suggested again.

**Can I undo a contact merge?**
Yes. Keeproll writes a backup (`.vcf`) before any contact change and offers it on the Summary screen. Open the file on your iPhone to restore the originals.

**Where are vault photos?**
Only on this iPhone, inside Keeproll, protected by Face ID or your passcode. They are not in iCloud or your photo library. Removing one from the vault is permanent, so save it back to Photos first if you want to keep it.

**The scan is slow the first time.**
The first scan analyses every photo on the device. After that, results are cached and a rescan takes seconds.
